About Us

Moving what businesses
and families need.

G. Maga Import & Export Nig. Ltd. sources and supplies quality vehicles, heavy-duty equipment, and general goods — helping businesses and individuals get durable, reliable products at prices that make sense.

Our Focus

Quality first, every time.

We work with trucks, tippers, excavators and private cars — each one checked for condition before it's offered to a customer. Alongside vehicles and machinery, we supply a wide range of goods in bulk or as single retail pieces, for business and personal needs alike.

Whether you're outfitting a construction site, growing a logistics fleet, or simply need one reliable vehicle, we treat every order — large or small — with the same standard.
